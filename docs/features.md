# Narwhal Assistant Features

## Search discovery

Helps you narrow the path to what matters faster by making the discovery process more structured and less noisy.

## Lightweight workflow

Built for users who want practical support without the overhead of large, complicated productivity tools.

## Focused navigation

Designed to help you stay centered on your current objective while reducing friction between idea and action.

## Productivity-first design

The experience is intentionally simple so you can focus on results instead of configuration.

## Best fit

Narwhal Assistant is most useful for:

- knowledge workers
- researchers
- fast-moving creators
- users doing rapid idea and task exploration
- anyone who wants a minimalist discovery workflow

## Summary

Narwhal Assistant is not meant to be a bloated platform. It is designed to be a focused tool that helps you discover, narrow, and act on information with less friction.
